# Third-party assets

## Surfaces

The wood, cloth, leather, bark and mud textures in `assets/tex` are photo-scanned PBR materials from **Poly Haven**
(https://polyhaven.com): `old_wood_floor`, `rough_wood`, `weathered_planks`, `dark_wood`, `rough_linen`,
`quatrefoil_jacquard_fabric`, `brown_mud_03`, `bark_brown_02`, `brown_leather`. They are released under
**CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); no attribution is required.

## Voices

The witch's screams and the reader's murmur in `assets/audio` are recordings from **Freesound** (https://freesound.org),
all released by their authors under **CC0 1.0** (no attribution required); they were trimmed, levelled and, for the murmur,
re-cut into a loop:

| file | Freesound sound |
| --- | --- |
| `shriek_1.ogg` | "Woman Screaming.wav" (#618193) |
| `shriek_2.ogg` | "Demonic Woman Scream.wav" by nick121087 (#333832) |
| `shriek_3.ogg` | "scream_female_dry" (#844112) |
| `shriek_4.ogg` | "woman scream.wav" (#609973) |
| `wail.ogg` | "Witch Death" (#218193) |
| `snarl.ogg` | "Loud Witch Scream" (#724988) |
| `murmur.ogg` | "Indistinct deep male mumble" (#536967) |

## Characters

The character models, textures and motion-capture animations in `assets/avatars` and `assets/anims` are derived from the
**Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library** (https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox), converted to compact Godot resources
(textures downscaled, meshes and animations re-baked; re-textured). Four avatars (the witch and three men, the men re-coloured) and the female and male animation sets ship with this game. They are used under the MIT License below.
